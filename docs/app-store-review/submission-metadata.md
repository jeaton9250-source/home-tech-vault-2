## Description

Your home remembers more than you think.

Home Tech Vault gives the technology and important records in your home one calm, private place to live. Save the details you will need later—without digging through drawers, inboxes, or old photos.

REMEMBER EVERY DEVICE

Keep model and serial numbers, purchase dates, warranty coverage, photos, locations, and notes with each device.

KEEP DOCUMENTS WITH THEIR STORY

File manuals, receipts, warranties, invoices, and whole-home documents. Connect each record to the right device or keep it with the home itself.

LET YOUR HOME REMIND YOU

Track recurring care and receive optional reminders before maintenance is due or when something becomes overdue.

ADD DETAILS FASTER

Scan a barcode or device label to fill in the details you can, then review everything before it becomes part of your vault.

YOUR HOME, WHEREVER YOU NEED IT

Changes synchronize with your Home Tech Vault web account so your records stay consistent across devices.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your signed-in session is stored securely, Face ID Vault Lock is optional, and a built-in demo household lets you explore without using personal information.
