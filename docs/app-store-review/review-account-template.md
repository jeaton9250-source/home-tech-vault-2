# Permanent App Review Account

Do not commit the review password. Store the credentials only in the team's password manager and the App Review Information fields in App Store Connect.

## Recommended identity

- Email: `appreview@hometechvault.com` or another monitored company-owned address
- Name: `Apple Reviewer`
- Household: `Maple Street Home`
- Account type: Standard homeowner
- MFA: Disabled for this account
- Account expiration: None

## Recommended sample content

Keep this account stable throughout review and future app updates.

- 4–6 devices across multiple rooms
- At least one device image
- At least one serial number and purchase date
- One active warranty and one expired warranty
- One PDF manual, one receipt image, and one whole-home document
- One upcoming care task within 48 hours
- One overdue care task
- At least five notifications, including one unread item

## Final verification

- Credentials work in a clean TestFlight installation.
- No email confirmation or one-time code is required.
- The account is not rate-limited, suspended, or scheduled for deletion.
- Sample documents open without asking for a separate login.
- The account contains fictional information only.
